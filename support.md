# Marginalia – Support

Questions, bugs or feature requests: open an issue at https://github.com/iflorit/marginalia/issues or email ismael@florit.es.

Frequently asked:
- **How do I add a book?** Library → + → pick an EPUB or PDF from Files, or use "Open in Marginalia" from any app.
- **Where are my notes?** Next to the text in the margin (iPad, iPhone Duo open) or behind each highlight (iPhone). Reader → Notes list shows them all; Share exports Markdown.
- **Restore my Remove Ads purchase:** Settings → Restore purchases.
- **iCloud sync:** Settings → iCloud Sync; notes and positions sync, book files do not (re-import the file on the other device).
